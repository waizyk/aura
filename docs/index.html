How Kelly works (60 seconds)

Kelly % = W − (1 − W) ÷ B
W = win rate, B = average win ÷ average loss

Example: W = 0.315, B = 2.8 → 0.315 − 0.685 ÷ 2.8 = 7.0%. Full Kelly maximises growth in theory, but it swings 50%+ in practice and your W and B are only estimates. Pros bet ¼ Kelly (here about 1.75%) and cap risk at 2%. If Kelly comes out negative, the system has no edge: don't trade it.
